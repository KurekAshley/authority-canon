# The 3 Keys to Success™

## Definition
A sequential formula for initiating and sustaining momentum through absolute decision, daily commitment, and strategic investment.

## Components
1. **Decision:** Must, not should. Felt, not intellectual.
2. **Commitment:** Consistency and daily application.
3. **Investment:** Time, money, and education in self.

## Function
Breaks paralysis and initiates momentum in any area of life or business.

## Application Context
Used when a founder is stuck in the knowing-doing gap and needs to shift from passive intention to active execution.

## What It Replaces
Goal-setting without identity alignment; "I'll try" mentality.
