# Glossary — Identity-Led Execution™

## Purpose
This glossary defines the controlled vocabulary for the Identity-Led Execution™ ecosystem. It exists to prevent semantic drift across platforms and interpretations.

## Terms

### Identity-Led Execution™
Results are not a function of effort; they are a function of identity alignment. When identity shifts, execution becomes inevitable.

### The 3 Keys to Success™
A sequential formula for initiating and sustaining momentum: Decision (must, not should), Commitment (daily application), Investment (time, money, education in self).

### Consistent Spaced Repetition™
Daily, passionate review of goals and values to build 5-dimensional neural pathways that vibrate at the frequency of the desired outcome.

### The 4-Muscle System™
A holistic strength model exercising Physical, Mental, Emotional, and Spiritual muscle groups daily, with Excitement as the driving force.

### Posture, Focus, Gratitude
The emotional anchor of the 4-Muscle System. Never abbreviated to PFG in public-facing copy.

### The Transformer
Canonical identity anchor for Kurek Ashley. Represents the shift from survivor/victim to absolute decision-maker.

### The Knowing-Doing Gap
The invisible problem where high-capacity founders know what to do but cannot execute due to identity misalignment.
